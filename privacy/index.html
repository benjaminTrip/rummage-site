2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and a password (we store it hashed, never in plain text). Your name, if you choose to give it.
  • Preferences: your cooking level, default servings and language.
  • Allergies and dietary preferences (for example vegan, halal, kosher, gluten-free). See "Sensitive information" below.
  • Your kitchen: pantry items and use-by dates, saved recipes, collections, meal plans and shopping lists.
  • Photos you choose to take or upload: of your fridge, cupboard or a receipt (to identify ingredients), of a recipe (to import it), of a dish you're cooking (to get feedback), and photos you add to your cooking journal.
  • Voice: if you use voice search or hands-free cooking, what you say.
  • Journal entries: ratings and notes about meals you've cooked.
  • Links you import from recipe websites or social posts.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information about how you use the app (which features, how often), tied to an anonymous account ID rather than your name or email, and only if you opt in. [See "Analytics" below.]
  • Technical information: device type, app version, and standard server logs (including IP address) used for security and fixing faults.
  • Purchase information: whether you subscribe and until when. We never see your card details; Apple handles payment.

3. Sensitive information (allergies and dietary preferences)

Allergies are health information, and dietary choices such as halal or kosher can reveal religious belief. The law treats both as "special category" data and requires your explicit consent to use them.

We ask for them only so we can avoid recipes that don't suit you. We collect them only with your explicit consent: the app asks before it saves your first allergy or dietary preference, and keeps a record of when you agreed. If you say no, nothing is saved and the app carries on. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing them in Profile; once you have removed them all, the record of your consent is cleared too, and we will stop using them. The app works without them, though recipes will then not be filtered for you.

Allergy filtering helps, but it is not a guarantee. See our Terms of Service. Always check ingredient labels yourself.

5. AI processing

Rummage uses AI to read your photos, write recipes and answer questions while you cook. To do this, we send the relevant information to AI providers (listed in section 6). Specifically:

  • Photos you submit for scanning, importing or cook feedback, together with context that helps the result — your cooking level, language, and, if you have set them, your allergies and dietary preferences — are sent to our AI provider for analysis.
  • Recipe titles and descriptions are sent to image-generation providers to create dish pictures. We do not intend these to contain personal data.
  • We do not send your name or email address to AI providers.
  • AI can make mistakes. Results are suggestions, not facts.

We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • See the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct anything that is wrong;
  • Delete your data (you can delete your account in the app: Profile → Account);
  • Restrict or object to how we use it;
  • Move your data to another service (portability);
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these, email support@rummage.kitchen. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls on the database so you can only reach your own data, and private storage for your photos. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to risk your rights, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

11. Analytics

Analytics are off unless you say yes. After you finish setting up, the app asks once whether you'd like to share anonymous usage data. If you decline, or dismiss the question, nothing is collected and nothing is stored on your device for analytics.

If you agree, we use PostHog (EU) to see which features are used. It stores an identifier on your device and links events to an anonymous account ID, not your name or email. We do not record the content of your recipes, searches or photos in analytics.

You can change your mind at any time in Profile → Privacy & legal → Share anonymous usage data. Turning it off stops collection immediately and resets the identifier on your device.
