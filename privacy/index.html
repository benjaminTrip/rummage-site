2. What we collect

You need an email address and password to have an account. Everything else is optional; without it, some features will not work (for example, recipes cannot be filtered for allergies you have not told us about).

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and a password (we store it hashed, never in plain text). Your name, if you choose to give it.
  • Preferences: your cooking level, default servings and language.
  • Allergies and dietary preferences (for example vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free). See "Sensitive information" below.
  • Your kitchen: pantry items and use-by dates, saved recipes, collections, meal plans and shopping lists.
  • Photos you choose to take or upload: of your fridge, cupboard or a receipt (to identify ingredients), of a recipe (to import it), of a dish you're cooking (to get feedback), and photos you add to your cooking journal. Fridge, receipt, "Snap a dish" and feedback photos are not kept by us. Cooking-journal photos are stored privately. A photo you add as the picture on a recipe you write yourself is stored in a public image folder, so anyone who has its web address can view it. Do not use a photo you would not want others to see.
  • Voice: if you use voice search, the text of what you say (see section 6 for how your device may process the audio).
  • Journal entries: ratings and notes about meals you've cooked.
  • Links you import from recipe websites and Pinterest, and screenshots or photos of recipes you import (sent for analysis, not kept).

Information collected automatically

  • Technical information: device type, app version, and standard server logs (including IP address) used for security and fixing faults.
  • Website: rummage.kitchen is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses. We set no cookies and run no analytics on the website or in the app.
  • Purchase information: whether you subscribe and until when. We never see your card details; Apple handles payment.

3. Sensitive information (allergies and dietary preferences)

Allergies are health information, and some dietary choices can hint at health or belief. The law treats this as "special category" data and requires your explicit consent to use it.

We ask for them only so we can avoid recipes that don't suit you. We collect them only with your explicit consent: the app asks before it saves your first allergy or dietary preference, and keeps a record of when you agreed. If you say no, nothing is saved and the app carries on. You can withdraw consent at any time by removing them in Profile; once you have removed them all, the record of your consent is cleared too, and we will stop using them. The app works without them, though recipes will then not be filtered for you. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew it.

Allergy filtering helps, but it is not a guarantee. See our Terms of Service. Always check ingredient labels yourself.

5. AI processing

Rummage uses AI to read your photos, write recipes and answer questions while you cook. To do this, we send the relevant information to AI providers (listed in section 6). Specifically:

  • Photos you submit (to identify ingredients, read a receipt, import a recipe, identify a dish, or get feedback while cooking) are sent to our AI provider for analysis, with your language and, where relevant, your cooking level.
  • Recipe requests: when we write or adapt recipes, we send the ingredients involved, any dish you searched for or question you typed, your cooking level and serving size, a short summary of the cuisines and dishes you have cooked and rated, and, if you have given consent, your allergies and dietary preferences. Your allergies and diet are also sent when you use "Snap a dish".
  • Recipe titles and descriptions are sent to image-generation providers to create dish pictures. We do not intend these to contain personal data.
  • We do not send your name or email address to AI providers.
  • AI can make mistakes. Results are suggestions, not facts.

We do not use AI to make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Anthropic's and Google's terms for their paid APIs say data sent through them is not used to train their models. We send image providers (Google and fal.ai) only a dish's title and description.

  • Apple (and on Android, Google) may process your voice to turn it into text when you use voice search. Depending on your device settings this happens on the device or on Apple's (or Google's) servers. Rummage receives only the text, not the audio.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, to protect our rights, or if the business is sold (in which case the new owner must honour this policy).

7. Transfers outside the UK

Some providers are in the United States. Where we transfer data outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards — for example the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or the provider's certification under the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. In practice: Google relies on its Data Privacy Framework certification (including the UK extension); Anthropic, RevenueCat and fal.ai rely on standard contractual clauses with the UK addendum (or the UK IDTA); Supabase relies on the standard contractual clauses in its data processing agreement; ImprovMX relies on the standard contractual clauses in its data processing agreement. Resend sends our account emails from the EU. You can contact us for a copy of the safeguards.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can:

  • See the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct anything that is wrong;
  • Delete your data (you can delete your account in the app: Profile → Account);
  • Restrict or object to how we use it;
  • Move your data to another service (portability);
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it.

To use any of these, email support@rummage.kitchen. We will reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access controls on the database so you can only reach your own data, and private storage for your journal photos. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to risk your rights, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

11. Analytics

Rummage does not use analytics. We do not collect usage statistics about how you use the app, and the app contains no analytics or advertising software. If that ever changes, we will ask for your permission first and update this policy before it takes effect.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
