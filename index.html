Rummage

Rummage turns what's in your kitchen into dinner. Photograph your fridge, freezer or cupboard, and Rummage works out what you've got and suggests recipes to match, at the level you like to cook. It also keeps track of what needs using up, plans your week and builds your shopping list.

Rummage is made for UK households and is coming to the App Store and Google Play.
