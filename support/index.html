Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the app: Profile → Account → Delete account. You can also ask by email. See Delete your account.

How do I cancel my subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by Rummage, so cancelling has to be done with them: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your Rummage account does not cancel it.

I paid but my plan hasn't updated. In the app, open the plan screen and choose Restore purchases. If that doesn't fix it, email us from the address you signed up with.

How do I turn off usage analytics? Profile → Privacy & legal → Share anonymous usage data.

Is the allergy filter a guarantee? No. It helps, but always check ingredient labels yourself. See our Terms of Service.
